No OSD voltage means no flight. This view keeps only the samples where the quad is flying: OSD gaps, the resting battery before takeoff and after landing are cut out, so the flights sit back to back. The line is a low-pass filter over flight time that fades readings in after each pause, so the higher voltage of a rested battery does not show up as a spike.
